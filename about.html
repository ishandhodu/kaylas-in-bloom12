About

Kayla & Bloom is a student-run floral studio creating fresh, thoughtful arrangements for life's moments big and small. From a single bud vase on a dorm nightstand to a full sorority house installation, every stem is chosen and arranged by hand with care.

Meet Kayla

Kayla & Bloom started in a college dorm room — Kayla arranging bouquets for friends and sorority sisters between classes, then posting the results on Instagram. What began as a few orders from people down the hall grew into a small studio, but it's still just her: she takes every order, sources every stem, and arranges every bouquet herself.

She built the brand around what she already loved — soft pinks, hand-drawn swans and florals, and a look that feels personal rather than polished-corporate. That's on purpose: she wants people to feel like they're ordering from her, not from a company.

— Kayla
